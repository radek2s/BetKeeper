
# Create bet with Justin

**Main task**:  
You are playing Fifa tournament and you want to make a bet with your friend Justin who will won final match John or Ben? You claim that John will win. You both agreed that winner gains a free ticket to cinema. Justin use email address justin@just.it. Invite Justin to participate to your bet.

**Expected time**:   
up to 5 minutes   
_(including pending time for admin actions, and Justin actions)_

**Research goal**  
Check if users is able to use application without any tutorial and special training. Assue that user interface is intuitive and allow to perform main tasks by expected users.
